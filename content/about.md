<h2>Description</h2>
<p>
  <em>Victor's Archive</em> is a digital archive featuring free streaming of original hypnotic rock and metal music by Victor's solo project <a href="https://reenchantment.band">Reenchantment</a>, along with content on other topics of interest. The website is available at <a href="https://victorsarchive.com">victorsarchive.com</a>. Reenchantment's digital press kit can be viewed at the <a href="https://dpk.reenchantment.band">dpk.reenchantment.band</a> and the music is released via <a href="https://paramusicology.com/">Paramusicology</a>, which is Victor's record label.
</p>

<h2>Biography</h2>
<p>
  Victor Fisher is a <em>musician</em> and <em>archivist</em> with multidisciplinary interests and a passion for learning. He is the founder of <a href="https://victorsarchive.com/">victorsarchive.com</a> (archive), <a href="https://reenchantment.band">Reenchantment</a> (band), and <a href="https://paramusicology.com/">Paramusicology</a> (label). He holds a Bachelor of Arts degree in music and religion, and a certificate in web development.
</p>
